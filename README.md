## The joke

> Dad left me a house and a drawer of unlabelled keys. He could've mentioned the inheritance was an escape room.

One line, 110 characters. A dad leaves his kid a house — and a drawer full of keys with no labels. The twist: the "inheritance" turns out to be an escape room, and dad forgot to mention it.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single joke to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT/entry.txt` when set, for local testing). No arguments, no network, no dependencies — just Node.

```sh
node main.ts
```
